FAQ

Do I need to tell the people in my meeting?

Yes. You must tell meeting participants you are using a transcription assistant, where the law requires it. You are responsible for getting consent.

What data is sent to the AI?

Audio stays on your PC. The text of the recent conversation, and any question you type, is sent to an AI provider to write the answer. We store only your device ID, usage counts, license information and form submissions. We do not store meeting content. See the privacy policy.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. You can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase. See the terms.

Windows shows a SmartScreen warning. Is the app safe?

New apps can trigger the Windows SmartScreen warning. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". The app is installed from the official GitHub releases page.

Does it work with Zoom?

Yes. It works with Microsoft Teams and Zoom on Windows, and with other meeting apps that play audio through your PC.
