AI meeting assistant FAQ

Is my meeting audio uploaded anywhere?

No. Meeting transcription runs locally on your PC with Whisper, so audio never leaves your computer. Only the text of the recent conversation is sent to an AI service to write an answer or summary. We do not store meeting content.

Does it work with headphones?

Yes. The app captures the audio playing on your PC and your microphone, so headphones do not affect it.

Does it work with Microsoft Teams and Zoom?

Yes. It works with Teams, Zoom, Google Meet in a browser and other meeting apps that play audio through your PC. No bot joins the call and no plugin is needed.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Your first meeting is free, with no card and no API keys. After that, plans start at $5 per month depending on your region and include 2,000 answers per month.

Does it save a transcript and meeting summary?

Yes. After each meeting it saves the full transcript on your PC and an AI meeting summary with key points, decisions and action items.

Can it tailor answers to my job?

Yes. The About me setting lets you describe your role, so answers use the right level of detail and wording for your field.

Is it an Otter.ai alternative?

It is an alternative for Windows users who want local transcription, no bot in the call and live answers. See the comparison on the Otter.ai alternative page.

Do I need to tell the people in my meeting?

Yes. You must tell meeting participants you are using a transcription assistant, where the law requires it. You are responsible for getting consent.

What data is sent to the AI?

Audio stays on your PC. The text of the recent conversation, and any question you type, is sent to an AI provider to write the answer. We store only your device ID, usage counts, license information and form submissions. We do not store meeting content. See the privacy policy.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. You can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase. See the terms.

Windows shows a SmartScreen warning. Is the app safe?

New apps can trigger the Windows SmartScreen warning. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". The app is installed from the official GitHub releases page.

Does it work with Zoom?

Yes. It works with Microsoft Teams and Zoom on Windows, and with other meeting apps that play audio through your PC.
