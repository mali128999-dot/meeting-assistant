Otter AI Alternative for Windows

Otter.ai is a well-known AI note taker, and for many teams it works well. If you searched for an Otter.ai alternative, you probably have a specific reason: you do not want a bot joining your calls, you want your audio to stay on your PC, or you want help while the meeting is still going on. Meeting Assistant is a Windows app built around those three points.

Is Otter AI free? And why look for an Otter AI alternative?

Otter AI offers a free plan with usage limits, and paid plans for more minutes and features; check otter.ai for their current limits and prices. People usually look for an Otter AI alternative when the free plan runs out, when their company does not allow a bot in calls, or when they want help during the meeting rather than notes afterwards. Meeting Assistant gives you one free meeting to try it, then plans from $5 per month depending on your region.

How the two approaches differ

Otter is a cloud service. Its meeting assistant typically joins a call as a participant, or processes recordings in the cloud, and you read the notes afterwards in its web or mobile apps. Meeting Assistant is a desktop app. It captures the audio that plays through your PC, plus your microphone, and turns it into text on your own computer with Whisper. Only the text is sent to an AI service, and only to write an answer or a summary.

When Meeting Assistant is a good fit

  • You work on a Windows laptop and join meetings in Teams, Zoom or a browser.
  • You wear headphones. The app captures the audio on your PC, so headphones are fine.
  • You need to keep client or internal audio off third-party servers.
  • You want an answer or a reminder in the middle of a discussion, not only notes afterwards.
  • You want a free AI note taker to try before paying anything.

When Otter may suit you better

Be honest about your needs. If you mostly use a Mac or a phone, want a shared web workspace for a whole team, or need a bot that records meetings you are not attending, a cloud service like Otter is built for that. Meeting Assistant runs on Windows only, and it works when you are in the meeting yourself.

Live answers, not just notes

The biggest practical difference is the live window. When someone asks a question, a suggested answer appears within a few seconds. You can also press F8 to ask now, or type a private question. An "About me" setting lets you describe your role so the answers fit your work. After the call, the full transcript and an AI summary are saved on your PC.

Pricing

Your first meeting is free, with no card and no API keys. After that, a monthly plan costs $5, $10 or $15 depending on your region, and includes 2,000 answers per month on up to 2 PCs. For Otter, check their site for current pricing, since plans change.
