Teams Meeting Transcription & AI Notes

Microsoft Teams has built-in transcription, but it depends on your organisation's licence and settings, and the host usually has to switch it on. Meeting Assistant takes a different route. It runs on your own Windows PC, listens to the meeting audio, and gives you a transcript, live AI answers and an AI notetaker for Teams without asking anyone to change a setting.

How it works with Microsoft Teams

Meeting Assistant does not join the Teams call. There is no bot in the participant list, and it does not need a Teams app, a plugin or admin approval. Instead, it captures the sound that Windows plays to your speakers or headphones, which is the voices of the other people, and your microphone, which is your own voice. The two sources are kept apart, so the transcript shows who said what: "them" or "me".

  1. Start the appOpen Meeting Assistant before or during your Teams meeting. A small window stays on top.
  2. Join as usualUse Teams normally, with speakers or headphones. Nothing else to configure.
  3. Read and askLive transcription appears, and answers show up when someone asks a question.

Speech to text on Windows, done locally

Transcription uses Whisper running on your own computer. This is speech to text for Windows that does not upload your audio anywhere. Only the text of the recent conversation is sent to an AI service when it has to write an answer or a summary. That matters if your calls include client details, project data or internal discussions.

Works with headphones

Because the app captures audio on the PC rather than in the room, headphones do not break it. You can sit in a quiet office or a noisy site and still get a clean transcript of what the other side says.

Live AI answers in Teams meetings

Teams transcription is usually read after the meeting. Meeting Assistant also helps during it. When a colleague asks a technical question, the answer appears in the window within seconds. Press F8 to ask on demand, F9 to pause, or type a private question that nobody else sees. The "About me" setting tells the AI your role, so answers are written for your field.

Notes after the meeting

When the call ends, the full transcript is saved on your PC together with an AI meeting summary: a short overview, key points, decisions and action items. Read more about AI meeting minutes and summaries.

Also works beyond Teams

The same method works for Zoom, Google Meet in a browser, and any other app that plays audio through your PC. See how it compares with a cloud note taker on the Otter.ai alternative page.

Be open with your participants

Where the law or your company policy requires it, tell people you are using a transcription assistant and get their consent. You are responsible for that.
