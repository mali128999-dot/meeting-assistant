Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-27

This policy explains what Meeting Assistant does with your data. In short: your audio stays on your PC, and we keep only the minimum needed to run your license.

1. Audio is processed locally

Meeting Assistant transcribes speech on your PC using Whisper. Audio is not uploaded to us or to any AI provider.

2. Text sent to AI providers

To write an answer, the app sends the text of the recent conversation (the transcript) and any question you type to an AI provider. Providers used include:

  • Groq
  • Google Gemini (the free tier may use data to improve models)
  • Z.ai (servers located in China)
  • OpenRouter
  • NVIDIA

The provider used for a given request may change depending on availability. Each provider processes the text under its own terms and privacy policy.

3. Data we store

We store only the following:

  • A device ID, derived from identifiers on your PC, used to enforce the trial and device limits.
  • Usage counts, such as the number of answers used per day and per month.
  • License information, such as your license key status, plan and expiry date.
  • Form submissions you send us through this website or the app, such as your name, email, country, company, phone and message.

We do not store meeting content, transcripts or audio.

4. Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Form submissions are sent to our backend service, which stores them so we can reply to you. We do not use advertising trackers on this site.

5. How long we keep data

We keep form submissions and license records only as long as needed to provide the service, answer your request, and meet legal obligations. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the EU and UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing done before you withdrew it;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Your responsibility for meeting participants

Meeting Assistant processes the conversation in your meetings. You must tell meeting participants that you are using a transcription assistant, where the law requires it.

8. Contact

To use any of your rights, or to ask a question about this policy, use the contact form on our website.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
